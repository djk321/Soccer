01
Rondo (4 v 1 Keep-Away)
👥 5 players · 10×10 yard square
Group Passing
Four players keep the ball away from one defender in the middle. The best warm-up in soccer — quick passing, angles, and first touch.
You
Teammates
Defender
Setup
Mark a 10×10 yard square with four cones. One attacker on each side, one defender in the middle, one ball.
How to Run
  1. Attackers pass around the square, moving along their side to stay open.
  2. The defender tries to touch or intercept the ball.
  3. Whoever loses the ball (or kicks it out) swaps into the middle.
  4. Bonus point for a "split pass" through the middle past the defender.
Focus Points
  • Open your body so you can see the next pass before the ball arrives.
  • Two touches max — one to control, one to pass.
  • Move to make a passing angle; don't hide behind the defender.

02
Give-and-Go to Goal
👥 2 players · Top of the box
Partner Passing Shooting
Pass to a partner, sprint past a cone "defender," get the ball right back in stride, and finish.
You
Partner
Cone
Setup
You start about 30 yards from goal with a ball. A cone stands in for a defender; your partner waits off to the side as the "wall."
How to Run
  1. Dribble toward the cone.
  2. Just before it, pass firmly to your partner (the "give").
  3. Sprint past the cone on the other side (the "go").
  4. Partner returns the ball one-touch into your path; you shoot. Switch roles after 5.
Focus Points
  • Accelerate the instant you release the pass.
  • Partner: play the return into space ahead of the runner, not to their feet.
  • Shoot early — low and to the corner.

03
Cone Slalom
🧍 1 player · 6 cones in a line
Solo Dribbling
Weave through a line of cones keeping the ball close — the classic way to build close control with both feet.
You
Cones
Setup
Six cones in a straight line, about 1–2 yards apart (closer = harder).
How to Run
  1. Dribble through the cones, weaving left and right.
  2. At the end, turn and come back.
  3. Round 1: right foot only. Round 2: left foot only. Round 3: inside and outside of both feet.
  4. Time yourself once you're comfortable.
Focus Points
  • Many small touches — keep the ball within a step of you.
  • Eyes up between cones whenever you can.
  • Stay on the balls of your feet with knees bent.

04
Passing Gates
👥 2 players · Open area with cone gates
Partner Passing First Touch
Partners move around a field of small cone "gates," scoring a point for every pass through one.
You
Partner
Gates
Setup
Set up 4–6 "gates" (two cones about 2 yards apart) spread around a 30×30 yard area. One ball per pair.
How to Run
  1. Pass through a gate to your partner on the other side.
  2. Receiver takes a touch toward a different gate and dribbles there.
  3. Partner runs to the far side of the new gate to receive.
  4. Count gates in 60 seconds; can't use the same gate twice in a row.
Focus Points
  • Pass with the inside of the foot, ankle locked.
  • First touch out of your feet toward the next gate.
  • Talk — call which gate you're heading to.

05
1 v 1 to Goal
👥 2–3 players · Edge of the box
Partner Dribbling Shooting Defending
Attacker takes on a defender and tries to score. The most game-like duel there is — great for both players.
You (attacker)
Defender
Keeper
Setup
Attacker starts 30 yards out with the ball; defender 10 yards in front. Add a keeper if you have a third player.
How to Run
  1. Attacker dribbles at the defender at speed.
  2. Use a feint (body swerve, step-over, or drop of the shoulder) to unbalance them.
  3. Cut away into space and shoot.
  4. Defender scores a point for winning the ball or forcing it out. Swap every 5 reps.
Focus Points
  • Attack the defender's front foot — make them turn.
  • Change speed after the move; the burst is what beats them.
  • Defender: stay on your feet and show the attacker wide.

06
Receive, Turn & Shoot
👥 2 players · Top of the box
Partner Shooting First Touch
Take a pass with your back to goal, turn on your first touch, and shoot before a defender can close you down.
You
Feeder
Keeper
Setup
You stand at the top of the box facing away from goal. Feeder is 15–20 yards in front of you with a supply of balls. A cone marks an imaginary defender.
How to Run
  1. Check your shoulder to see where the cone (defender) is.
  2. Feeder passes firmly to your feet.
  3. Turn away from the cone with your first touch.
  4. Shoot with your second touch. 10 reps, then swap.
Focus Points
  • Open your hips as the ball arrives so your touch takes you toward goal.
  • Use the outside of the foot to turn one way, inside to turn the other.
  • Shoot across the keeper toward the far post.

07
Crossing & Finishing
👥 3–4 players · Wing and penalty box
Group Crossing Finishing
A winger dribbles down the line and crosses; two strikers time their runs to the near and far posts.
You (winger)
Strikers
Keeper
Setup
Winger starts wide near midfield with a ball. Two strikers wait about 25 yards from goal. Keeper in goal if available.
How to Run
  1. Winger dribbles hard down the sideline toward the goal line.
  2. Strikers wait, then sprint — one to the near post, one to the far post.
  3. Winger crosses into the space between the keeper and the strikers.
  4. Strikers finish first-time. Rotate positions and switch wings.
Focus Points
  • Strikers: arrive late and fast rather than standing and waiting.
  • Winger: look up before crossing; aim for space, not a player.
  • Finish down into the ground — low headers and side-foot volleys.

08
2 v 1 Overload
👥 3–4 players · Half field
Group Passing Shooting
Two attackers versus one defender. Learn exactly when to dribble and when to pass to make the 2-v-1 count.
You
Teammate
Defender
Setup
Two attackers start near midfield, about 15 yards apart. One defender starts 20 yards in front. Keeper optional.
How to Run
  1. Ball carrier dribbles straight at the defender.
  2. Teammate stays level and wide to give a passing option.
  3. If the defender steps to the ball, pass; if he covers the teammate, keep going and shoot.
  4. Defender scores by winning the ball. Rotate every rep.
Focus Points
  • Commit the defender before passing — pass too early and he can recover.
  • Teammate: don't run offside; stay level with the ball.
  • Finish quickly before help arrives.

09
Wall Ball
🧍 1 player · Any sturdy wall
Solo Passing First Touch
A wall is the most patient passing partner in the world. Pass, control, repeat — alternating feet.
You
Wall
Setup
Stand 5–10 yards from a solid wall (a schoolyard or handball court is perfect) with one ball.
How to Run
  1. Pass against the wall with your right foot; control the rebound.
  2. Pass with your left foot; control.
  3. Progress to one-touch passing, alternating feet.
  4. Step farther back and add a touch to the side before each pass.
Focus Points
  • Lock your ankle and follow through toward the target.
  • Cushion the rebound — let your foot "give" as it arrives.
  • Count passes in a row without a mistake and try to beat it.

10
Jockey & Shadow
👥 2 players · 15×20 yard channel
Partner Defending
Mirror a dribbler, stay goal-side, show them wide — and pounce on the heavy touch.
You (defender)
Attacker
Cone goal
Setup
A channel about 15 yards wide. The attacker starts at one end with the ball; you start in the middle. A two-cone goal sits behind you.
How to Run
  1. Attacker tries to dribble through the cone goal behind you.
  2. You stay goal-side, side-on, and backpedal at the attacker's pace.
  3. Steer them toward the sideline, away from the goal.
  4. When they take a heavy touch, step in and poke the ball away. Swap after each rep.
Focus Points
  • Low center of gravity — knees bent, weight on the balls of your feet.
  • Watch the ball, not the attacker's tricks.
  • Be patient; don't dive in.
